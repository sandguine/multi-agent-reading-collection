Preparation

The schedule assumes familiarity with proofs, linear algebra, calculus, elementary real analysis, and probability. Game-theoretic concepts are introduced as needed.

For reading preparation, begin with Shoham et al. and choose the anchor for the session you want to explore. The collection is more than a day’s reading.

Small-group work

The schedule highlights robust equilibrium certification (worksheet Exercise 1.1) and observational equivalence (Exercise 3.1). Allow about 20 minutes to work and 10 minutes to compare conclusions. The online collection includes shorter companion versions with collapsible solutions.
