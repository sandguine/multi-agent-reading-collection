Sources and editorial choices

The schedule’s bibliography supplies 42 references. All appear once in the reading collection. Wellman, Tuyls, and Greenwald’s Empirical Game-Theoretic Analysis: A Survey is the sole added reference, drawn from the supplied slides and worksheet.

The six topic collections separate empirical game theory from normative agency. Lanctot et al. is placed with empirical games because of the PSRO connection; it remains a reading from the schedule’s stochastic-dynamics bibliography. Collection numbers refer to this guide, not a relabeling of the source timetable.

Reading priorities, estimated study times, questions, and annotations were prepared for this guide. They do not claim to be the lecturers’ own rankings. Time scores refer to the suggested target, not necessarily the complete paper or book. Essays, primary reports, empirical studies, and formal results are described according to their different roles.

The short exercises are companion material. Adaptations of the supplied worksheet and newly written discussion prompts are labeled individually. The original full worksheet remains available above.

Reference checks

Links and available abstracts or full texts were reviewed on 8 November 2026. Some publisher sites block automated access or return unreadable responses. Original citation links are retained, with access notes in each affected reading’s “Citation & source” disclosure; open alternatives are supplied where checked. An access failure is not evidence that a citation is invalid.

Bibliographic citations preserve the schedule’s publication information; displayed entries note consequential version differences, including the 2021 update to the provability-logic paper and the later edit to King’s essay. Publisher pages may change after this review.

About the format

The organization takes inspiration from Charles Renshaw-Whitman’s Ambitious Mechinterp and Activation Geometry reading collection: a guided sequence, independent importance and length scores, and exercises with hidden solutions. This site’s code, annotations, and organization were prepared separately for the multi-agent materials.
